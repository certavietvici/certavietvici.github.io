# certavietvici.github.io

This is a résumé/curriculum vitae GitHub Pages website using html for an experience DoW IT contractor seeking to move into formal subcontracting with prime contractors versus W2 or 1099 work. It demonstrates registration with sam.gov, completion of courses required for the roles needed to hold an FCL, and asserts extensive, specialized experience supporting DoW on 4 continents.

Style is minimalist with a banner similar to those found on Government networks, stating the principal purpose of the page, instead of a classification with caveats.

A custom domain is used: https://www.certavietvici.com

Certavi et vici is Latin for "I have fought and conquered" and is the motto of the Byrne clan of Ireland. The favicon is the Byrne crest without the mermaid holding a combn & mirror and in black & white instead of the red shield with white dexter hands and chevron, in keeping with the muted, monochrome, minimalist style.

Certification certificates are presented in .png format as their original .pdf files loaded slowly by comparison. The .png files are all screenshot captures of the original .pdf files.

A .pdf version of the main page is made available to download which is a 'fit to one page' print to .pdf.
